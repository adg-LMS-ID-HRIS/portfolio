Global Enterprise · Cornerstone LMS
Cornerstone IT Administration & Analytics
Challenge
A global organization of 130,000+ employees across multiple business units needed reliable, consistent LMS operations, clean learner data synchronized across three integrated HR systems (Cornerstone, Workday HCM, and PeopleSoft), and executive-ready compliance and workforce development reporting.
Solution
Serve as IT Cornerstone Administrator managing the full technical side of the platform — configuring Organization Units, Learning Objects, Channels, Pathways, and compliance curricula. Designed and maintain API integrations between Cornerstone and 20+ third-party systems, including data exchange specifications with Workday and PeopleSoft. Built Power BI dashboards surfacing training completion rates, compliance metrics, and L&D KPIs for executive and operational leadership. Developed standard operating procedures and admin governance documentation to ensure platform consistency across global teams.
Result
Single source of truth for learning data across 130,000+ global users. Leadership receives standardized monthly dashboards in place of ad-hoc data requests. Integration with 20+ third-party systems maintains learner data accuracy across all connected platforms.

Union-Aware T&L Training & Change Program

Solution
